# LinkedIn Post -- Nautilus Terminal Rebrand

---

We renamed our supply chain intelligence terminal. Not because the old name was taken -- but because the new one is more honest about what the product actually does.

The terminal started as a prototype built to answer a question I kept running into in procurement: why is it so hard to get a clear picture of what's happening in your supply chain right now? News is scattered. Risk signals are buried in reports nobody reads until it's too late. Sourcing decisions get made on gut instinct and last quarter's data.

The product was built to fix that. And as it grew, the original name stopped fitting what it had become.

We renamed it **Nautilus** -- a nod to Jules Verne's submarine, a vessel designed to move through the deep with precision, seeing what surface-level visibility misses. That's the product philosophy: go deeper than the headline, surface what actually matters for your supply chain.

Here's what Nautilus Terminal does today:

-- Real-time geopolitical risk monitoring across 40+ global sourcing hubs, with live port status and chokepoint alerts
-- AI-powered opportunity analysis: describe a sourcing problem and get a ranked hub breakdown, risk assessment, and strategic directive
-- Live freight intelligence covering trade corridors, sanctioned routes, and seasonal disruption patterns
-- Foreign exchange and commodity price feeds contextualized for procurement -- not just the number, but what it means for your costs
-- Mission history so every sourcing scan is saved and replayable
-- Google OAuth sign-in, legal pages, and a hardened API layer -- production-ready, not just a demo

The terminal is live at https://nautilus-terminal.vercel.app/terminal

If you work in procurement, trade compliance, or supply chain risk -- try it and tell me what's missing. I'd genuinely love feedback from people who deal with this problem every day.
