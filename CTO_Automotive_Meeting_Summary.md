**Bolster Packaging and Fit/Finish**

This was the most complex discussion of the meeting. The current bolster baseline only has 3 mm clearance to the door trim. Adding the 1 mm skin + 2 mm foam consumes all of that clearance, causing a clash at both the door trim and the pillar.

Dilip studied two scenarios:

1. **Follow baseline surface exactly** — the substrate ends up touching the door trim with no room for ribs or features.
2. **Offset the A-surface by 3 mm** — this clashes with the mating part and impacts flush/fit finish conditions.

Neither scenario works as-is. The proposed path forward is to move the bolster A-surface inboard — similar to the bump-out approach used on the armrest substrate — which would slightly shrink the B-side to recover gap without touching attachment points. Scotlen will sketch the section and evaluate; I'll do the same and align with studio.

It was also flagged that a full environment study is needed covering the B-pillar, sheet metal, door seals, and all mating parts. The production data did not include upholstered parts, so all clearances were originally set flush — this needs to be revisited end-to-end.
