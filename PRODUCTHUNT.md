## DESCRIPTION

Procurement teams spend hours hunting across 6 different tabs before they can even shortlist a supplier region. Duty rates here, freight costs there, compliance docs somewhere else, ESG data nowhere.

**Atlas Terminal collapses all of that into a 10-second scan.**

Type any material or component — IATF-certified brake pads, neodymium magnets, semiconductor wafers, food-grade soy — and Atlas maps the best global sourcing hubs with:

- 📦 Customs & duty rates by HS code
- 🚢 Freight costs and port wait times
- ⚖️ Compliance and regulatory flags
- 🌱 ESG ratings with sourcing notes
- 💱 Live FX snapshot for cost modelling
- 📄 One-click PDF mission brief for your team
- 🔗 Shareable scan links (just send the URL)

Built for sourcing managers, procurement leads, and supply chain teams at manufacturers, OEMs, and anyone buying globally at scale.

**It's free. No signup required. Try it now.**
