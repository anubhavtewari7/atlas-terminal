## Maker First Comment (pin this immediately after launch)

---

Hey Product Hunt 👋

I built Atlas Terminal because supply chain teams I've worked with were spending days on tasks that should take minutes — stitching together data from 10 different tabs to answer a single sourcing question.

**The problem:** Tariff changes, export controls, port disruptions, and FX swings hit at random. By the time most teams find out, the damage is done. Existing tools are either expensive enterprise platforms ($50k+/yr) or outdated static databases.

**What I built:** A real-time strategic intelligence HUD. You type a product — like *"neodymium magnets for EV motor assembly"* — and get:

- 🌍 **Interactive globe** showing active sourcing hubs with green threat overlays
- ⚠️ **Live trade threats** (China rare earth export controls, Section 301 tariffs, critical mineral designations)
- 🏭 **6 sourcing hubs** with ESG scores, supplier contacts, and advisory data
- 📊 **Side-by-side region comparison** — tariff rates, freight costs, lead times, compliance notes
- 🔢 **HS/Tariff code lookup** with CBP duty rates and FTA schedules
- 💰 **Total Landed Cost calculator** pre-filled from scan data
- ⚓ **Port congestion monitor** — 20 global ports with wait times and alerts
- 🛡️ **Trade Lane Risk Score** — geopolitical, weather, labor, infrastructure breakdown
- ⚖️ **Incoterms 2020 calculator**
- 📋 **Compliance checklist generator**
- 💱 **Live FX rates** from ECB data (updates every hour)

**It's completely free, runs in your browser, no login required.**

I'd love feedback from anyone in ops, procurement, logistics, or trade compliance — especially:
- What sourcing decisions do you currently make without good data?
- What would make this useful enough to use daily?

Thanks for checking it out 🙏

🔗 https://atlas-terminal-tau.vercel.app
